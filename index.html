Chalk Landscapes & Woodwork

Well-crafted, considered spaces — built by hand, built to last.

We're a Hove-based team specialising in sustainable landscaping, garden woodwork and bespoke internal carpentry. From decking, fencing and paving to timber builds and interior joinery, every project gets the same thoughtful, hands-on approach.

We work carefully around what's already there — mature trees, established planting, the character of the house — and we're honest about cost from day one.

Fencing · Gate · Lawn

02

Design · Paths · Accessibility

Reclaiming a lost side garden

In a narrow garden, straight lines create a tunnel. We introduced a sweeping curve that draws the eye across the space — making it feel wider, calmer and far more open.

  • Cleared years of invasive growth and builder's rubble
  • Levelled with timber sleepers — flat and safe, designed with mobility in mind
  • Natural self-binding gravel with steel edging — firm underfoot, no heavy concrete
  • New posts, trellis and climbing wires ready for summer

Paving · Fencing · Hove

A bigger-feeling courtyard that kept its character

A tricky job — new boundaries installed around mature trees and established shrubs with real care and patience. The result is bright, modern and much larger-feeling.

  • Old brick planter removed to win back floor space
  • Patio extended with new red-toned paving
  • Tired trellis replaced with stained horizontal slatted fencing
  • Existing planting carefully tidied and protected

04

Turf · Lawn renovation

A lawn reborn after building work

A large extension and a scorching summer left nothing but bare, compacted ground. Eight days after laying, the new lawn was rooting fast and settling neatly against the paved edges.

  • Ground prepared and levelled after heavy construction
  • Premium turf and topsoil from a trusted local grower
  • Hand-laid around the koi pond and garden features
  • Ready for its first light cut at three weeks
05

Porch · Driveway · Fencing · Lawn

Front door to back fence — one team, every trade.

A full exterior renovation: the Victorian porch rebuilt with new structural timber, tiling, paint and guttering; a gravel driveway on a deep MOT sub-base with grano screed and gravel-mat stabilisation; new slatted fencing and a matching gate; and a mossy, uneven lawn stripped, levelled and returfed inside bespoke sleeper edging.
